D3: Sketchfab-derived Textured 3D Models (Public Subset)

This release contains the publicly redistributed Sketchfab-derived portion of D3. It includes one construction-site model and five parts of one street model, distributed as six textured OBJ meshes with MTL files and associated texture images.

Sources and attribution:

1. "Construction site, building site, architecture" by Metazeon, licensed under CC BY 4.0. No additional processing was reported for this release.
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/construction-site-building-site-architecture-62fc443a7d91465f844de1cf95731f60

2. "Small Canadian Street Experiment Photogrammetry" by Air Digital (@AirDigitalca), licensed under CC BY 4.0. The original model was partitioned into five parts using MeshLab, corresponding to fullstree_1 through fullstree_5.
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/small-canadian-street-experiment-photogrammetry-1bdfb91607ea4310a4bebdbf167714c7

The Google-sourced Berlin, Los Angeles, Paris, and Sydney scenes used in the full experimental D3 dataset are excluded from this public release. This item is the public subset and does not contain the complete experimental D3 dataset.

The archive includes a README, the full license text, third-party attribution notices, recorded source metadata, and a SHA-256 checksum manifest. Preserve the archive's directory structure when extracting it to maintain the model, material, and texture references. Please cite this Figshare item and retain the original-model attributions when reusing the data.
